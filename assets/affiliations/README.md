# Affiliation marks

These are cropped, rasterized copies used by the pdfLaTeX title slide. Their
official sources are:

- `layerzero-title.png`: [LayerZero](https://layerzero.network/images/logo-layerzero.png)
- `umd-title.png`: [University of Maryland brand assets](https://brand.umd.edu/logos)
- `ntt-title.png`: [NTT Research media resources](https://ntt-research.com/media-resources/)
- `sydney-title.png`: [University of Sydney site logo](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/icons/logos/logo-usyd-dark.svg)

The marks remain complete wordmarks; no seal or shield has been detached. The
University of Sydney's [logo guidance](https://www.sydney.edu.au/about-us/affiliates-and-contractors/logo-requests.html)
states that external use requires approval.
